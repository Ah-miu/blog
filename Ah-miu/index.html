What am I really waiting for?我到底在等什么？

A new machine, or AI's ability to perceive and act upon the physical world?一台新机器，还是 AI 感知并作用于物理世界的能力？

The devices around me already have cameras, microphones, motors, and various capabilities to sense and act upon the physical world.我身边的设备，其实早就拥有摄像头、麦克风、电机，以及各种感知和行动能力。

But having these capabilities doesn't mean AI can use them.但设备拥有这些能力，不代表 AI 就能使用这些能力。

They each sense and act on their own, but they're not connected into a system that can understand its surroundings and coordinate actions.它们各自感知、各自行动，却还没有连接成一个能理解环境、协调行动的系统。

So how can AI begin to use these existing capabilities?那么，如何让 AI 开始利用这些已经存在的能力？

I decided to start with cameras.我决定先从摄像头入手。

If multiple live camera views were connected, could they be coordinated and controlled as one?如果把多个实时视角连接起来，能不能统一调度它们？

That way, I could decide where AI looks.这样，我就能决定它看哪里。

But what if I took it a step further and let AI decide for itself?但如果更进一步，把决定权交给它呢？

We've seen what Ah-miu could do with a few devices working together.我们已经看到，Ah-miu 能让几台设备协同工作。

So I decided to put it to a harder test — an entire room.于是，我决定给它一次更严苛的考验——让整个房间的设备一起参与。

13 types of devices. 23 in total. One music clock.13 种设备，总共 23 台。共用一个音乐时钟。

Every camera handoff had to happen at the right moment. Every movement had to hit the beat.每一次镜头接力，都必须卡准时机。每一个动作，都必须精准踩在节拍上。

One missed cue or delayed video frame could throw the whole sequence out of sync.只要漏掉一条指令，或是某一路实时视频延迟一帧，就可能打乱整段拍摄的节奏。

Could one mind make an entire room of separate devices work as one, in real time?一个智能，能否让整个房间里原本独立的设备，实时协同，像一个整体一样行动？

Started the music clock with a 0.5 ms error.音乐时钟的启动误差仅为 0.5 毫秒。

Turned the room red on eight heartbeats, in step to 50 ms on every camera.让整个房间随着八次心跳节拍变为红色，各机位的同步误差均控制在 50 毫秒以内。

Fired 140 cues, none more than 19 ms late.触发 140 条演出指令，没有一条延迟超过 19 毫秒。

Aimed a phone at the arm to within 0.03°.让手机镜头对准机械臂，角度误差不超过 0.03°。

Cut away and back on two beats 0.42 seconds apart.在相隔仅 0.42 秒的两个节拍上，完成镜头的切出与切回。

Nodded the drone on two beats, each step within 20 ms.让无人机在两个节拍上完成点头动作，每次误差均控制在 20 毫秒以内。

We are constantly being asked to adapt to new technologies, new rules, and new divisions of labor in society. Each of us is like a brick, hurriedly moved, placed, and fitted by the times into a world that grows ever more efficient.我们不断被要求适应新的技术、新的规则、新的社会分工。每个人都像一块砖，被时代匆忙地搬运、安放、拼接进一个越来越高效的世界。

But people are not bricks.可是，人不是砖。

We have emotions that cannot be standardized, exhaustion that is difficult to put into words, and needs that are subtle and uniquely our own. Between the seemingly seamless structures of society, there are always gaps. Our loneliness, anxiety, and pain are often connected through these gaps, yet they are not always truly seen.我们有无法被标准化的情绪，有难以言说的疲惫，有细腻而独特的需要。那些看似严丝合缝的社会结构之间，其实始终存在着缝隙。我们的孤独、焦虑与痛苦，常常就在这些缝隙中彼此相连，却未必真正被看见。

And between people, there naturally exists another kind of distance.而人与人之间，也天然存在着另一种距离。

Even those closest to us cannot understand each other at every moment. Love needs closeness, but it also needs space. We long to be understood, but not intruded upon; we long for companionship, yet also need to preserve a space of our own.即使是最亲密的人，也无法时时刻刻理解彼此。爱需要靠近，也需要留白。我们渴望被理解，却不希望被侵入；渴望有人陪伴，也需要保有自己的空间。

It is precisely in these gaps that I hope Ah-miu can grow.正是在这些缝隙里，我希望 Ah-miu 能够生长出来。

Like a small blade of grass growing through a crack between bricks.就像砖缝里长出的一株草。

It does not seek to replace human relationships, nor does it try to fill every gap. It simply allows intelligence to flow around us in a softer, more natural way.它不试图替代人与人的关系，也不试图把每一道缝隙填满。它只是让智能以一种更柔软、更自然的方式，流动在我们身边。

A little closer to people, a little closer to the real and subtle needs of each individual.离人更近一点，离每个人真实而细微的需求更近一点。
